NOTE 1 - GOING CONCERN

The accompanying consolidated financial statements have been prepared assuming that the Company will continue as a going concern. As of February 28, 2026, the Company had negative cash flow from operating activities of $9,344,534. As of February 28, 2026 the Company has an accumulated deficit of $171,121,742 and negative working capital of $17,017,745. Management does not anticipate having positive cash flow from operations in the near future. These factors raise substantial doubt about the Company's ability to continue as a going concern for the twelve months following the issuance of these financial statements.
